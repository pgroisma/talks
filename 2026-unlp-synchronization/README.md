# Sincronización (Synchronization)

Pablo Groisman. 2nd Interdisciplinary Meeting on Physics and Mathematics (*2do Encuentro Interdisciplinario de Física y Matemática*), Universidad Nacional de La Plata, Argentina, 2026.

The slides are in Spanish.

[Slides (PDF)](SincronizacionUNLP.pdf)

## Videos

The [`videos/`](videos) folder contains the simulations shown in the talk. If you download this whole folder (the PDF together with `videos/`), the videos play from within the PDF in viewers that support embedded video, such as Okular or Adobe Acrobat. Third-party videos shown during the talk are not included.

| Video | What it shows |
|-------|---------------|
| `erdos_renyi.mp4` | An Erdős–Rényi random graph as the connection probability $p$ grows |
| `erdos_renyi_sync.mp4` | Fireflies on that graph synchronizing |
| `ola_estadio.mp4` | A traveling wave (twisted state) on a cycle |
| `rgg_epsilon.mp4` | A random geometric graph |
| `ola_lago.mp4` | Fireflies around a lake: a wave that does not die out |
| `ola_lago_q2.mp4` | The same, with a wave that winds twice |
| `cierre_pantalla_unlp.mp4` | Closing of the talk |

Music in the closing video: J. Brahms, *Hungarian Dance No. 5*. Fulda Symphonic Orchestra, conducted by Simon Schindler (EFF Open Audio License).
